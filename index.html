About us

A Colby carrier built on harvest seasons.

Family-run out of Colby in northwest Kansas, Hatfield Trucking has held USDOT registration since December 1998 and contract operating authority since November 2000.

Shippers, elevators and brokers get a carrier that knows the roads between the field, the elevator and the mill.
